# Eventurion — Interactive Historical Atlas of India (Bharatavarsha)

A high-end dark historical exploration website, interactive atlas, and digital research archive dedicated to the recorded history of India across 5,000 years — from the Indus Valley Civilization to the Indian National Freedom Movement.

## Complete Indian Historical Periods Covered

1. **Indus & Bronze Age (3300 BCE – 1500 BCE)**
   - Mature Harappan urban civilization (Mohenjo-daro, Harappa, Dholavira, Lothal).
   - Standardized weights & measures, underground drainage, and world's earliest tidal drydock.
   - Primary Records: Mesopotamian Cuneiform tablets of Sargon of Akkad, ASI Dholavira monographs.

2. **Vedic Era & 16 Mahajanapadas (1500 BCE – 345 BCE)**
   - The composition of the Rigveda, Samaveda, Yajurveda, Atharvaveda, and the Upanishads.
   - Emergence of the 16 Great Republics and Kingdoms (Magadha, Kosala, Vajji, Avanti, Kashi).
   - Primary Records: Rigveda Samhita manuscripts, Bimbisara and Ajatashatru royal epigraphs.

3. **Nanda Empire (345 BCE – 322 BCE)**
   - First imperial unification of Northern India under Mahapadma Nanda.
   - World's largest standing army in the 4th century BCE halting Alexander the Great's advance.
   - Primary Records: Diodorus Siculus, Curtius Rufus, and Purana chronicles.

4. **Maurya Empire (322 BCE – 185 BCE)**
   - Founded by Chandragupta Maurya and Chanakya (Kautilya).
   - Peak under Emperor Ashoka the Great; Kalinga War (261 BCE), ethical transformation, and Dhamma edicts.
   - Primary Records: Megasthenes' *Indica*, Kautilya's *Arthashastra*, Major Rock Edict XIII.

5. **Satavahana Empire (100 BCE – 225 CE)**
   - Indigenous Deccan masters, Great Stupa at Amaravati, Roman maritime trade, Shalivahana calendar.
   - Primary Records: Nashik Cave inscriptions of Queen Gautami Balashri, *Periplus of the Erythraean Sea*.

6. **Kushan Empire (30 CE – 375 CE)**
   - Crossroads of India, Central Asia, and Rome under Kanishka the Great.
   - Gandhara & Mathura art, Fourth Buddhist Council, gold dinar currency.
   - Primary Records: Rabatak Inscription, Kanishka casket.

7. **Gupta Empire — Classical Golden Age (319 CE – 543 CE)**
   - Inventions of zero, decimal notation, and trigonometry by Aryabhata; metallurgy of Delhi Iron Pillar; Kalidasa's Sanskrit epics; founding of Nalanda Mahavihara.
   - Primary Records: Prayag Prashasti (Allahabad Pillar) of Harishena, Faxian's travelogues.

8. **Empire of Harsha (606 CE – 647 CE)**
   - Imperial unification of North India from Kannauj, Buddhist assemblies, and Sanskrit drama.
   - Primary Records: Banabhatta's *Harshacharita*, Xuanzang's *Datang Xiyuji*.

9. **Chalukyas & Pallavas (543 CE – 897 CE)**
   - Pulakeshin II defeating Harsha on the Narmada River; rock-cut Shore Temple at Mahabalipuram.
   - Primary Records: Aihole Inscription of Ravikirti, Kuram copper plates.

10. **Rashtrakuta Empire (753 CE – 982 CE)**
    - World superpower recognized by Arab geographers (Sulaiman and Al-Masudi).
    - Monolithic Kailash Temple at Ellora; Amoghavarsha's *Kavirajamarga*.
    - Primary Records: *Silsilat al-Tawarikh*, Ellora Cave 16 plinth inscriptions.

11. **Imperial Chola Dynasty (848 CE – 1279 CE)**
    - All-granite Brihadisvara Temple, lost-wax Nataraja bronzes, 1025 CE naval conquest of Srivijaya.
    - Primary Records: Thanjavur temple granite epigraphs, Tiruvalangadu copper plates.

12. **Pala Empire of Bengal (750 CE – 1161 CE)**
    - Democratic election of Gopala, patron of Nalanda and Vikramashila universities.
    - Primary Records: Khalimpur copper plate charter.

13. **Delhi Sultanate (1206 CE – 1526 CE)**
    - Repelling 5 Mongol invasions under Alauddin Khilji, Qutb Minar, Tanka coinage.
    - Primary Records: Ziauddin Barani's *Tarikh-i-Firoz Shahi*.

14. **Vijayanagara Empire (1336 CE – 1646 CE)**
    - Golden epoch under Krishnadevaraya; Hampi capital, diamond markets, Vitthala stone chariot.
    - Primary Records: Domingo Paes & Fernão Nunes chronicles, Krishnadevaraya's *Amuktamalyada*.

15. **Rajput Kingdoms & Mewar (728 CE – 1818 CE)**
    - Chivalric resistance, Chittorgarh, Kumbhalgarh, Rana Sanga, Maharana Pratap & Battle of Haldighati.
    - Primary Records: *Rajprashasti Mahakavyam* marble slabs at Rajsamand.

16. **Ahom Kingdom of Assam (1228 CE – 1826 CE)**
    - 600 years of sovereignty, General Lachit Borphukan, 1671 Battle of Saraighat on the Brahmaputra.
    - Primary Records: Ahom Buranjis on Sanchi bark.

17. **Mughal Empire (1526 CE – 1857 CE)**
    - Babur, Akbar the Great, Shah Jahan; Taj Mahal, Red Fort, Mansabdari system.
    - Primary Records: *Ain-i-Akbari*, *Akbarnama*, *Baburnama*.

18. **Maratha Empire & Hindavi Swarajya (1674 CE – 1818 CE)**
    - Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj's coronation at Raigad, naval fortresses, Peshwa Baji Rao I.
    - Primary Records: *Sabhasad Bakhar*, *Adnyapatra* naval edict.

19. **Sikh Empire (1799 CE – 1849 CE)**
    - Maharaja Ranjit Singh, Hari Singh Nalwa at Khyber Pass, Golden Temple Amritsar.
    - Primary Records: Sohan Lal Suri's *Umdat-ut-Tawarikh*.

20. **First War of Independence & Freedom Movement (1857 CE – 1947 CE)**
    - 1857 Uprising (Rani Lakshmibai, Mangal Pandey), Satyagraha (Dandi Salt March), Netaji's INA, and August 15, 1947 Independence.
    - Primary Records: Provisional Government of Azad Hind Proclamation, Nehru's *Tryst with Destiny*.

## All Working Options & Features

- **Interactive Subcontinent Atlas Map**:
  - Pan, Zoom, Reset.
  - Ancient trade highways: Uttarapatha, Dakshinapatha, Maritime Spice Route.
  - Mountain topography (Himalayas, Western Ghats, Eastern Ghats, Vindhyas) and sacred rivers.
  - Live search bar and filter pills (Civilization, Empire, Battles/Events).
  - Continuous timeline scrubber from 3300 BCE to 1950 CE with Play/Pause auto-animation.
  - Active selection panel with "Explore Full Civilization Profile" and "AI Reconstruction".
- **Dynamic Civilization Profiles**:
  - Works for any civilization selected (Maurya, Gupta, Chola, Vijayanagara, Maratha, Mughal, Indus, Rajput, Sikh, Ahom, etc.).
  - Displays rulers, territory statistics, primary historical sources, and major events.
- **Dynamic Historical Event Pages**:
  - In-depth battlefield dossiers for Kalinga War, Battle of Ten Kings, Battle of Haldighati, Battle of Raichur, Battle of Saraighat, 1857 Uprising, Dandi March, etc.
  - "View on Atlas Map", "Explore Timeline", and "Open Civilization Profile".
- **AI Archaeological Reconstruction Studio**:
  - 6 reconstructable sites: **Pataliputra**, **Dholavira**, **Hampi**, **Nalanda**, **Thanjavur**, **Raigad**.
  - Toggles for Architecture, Environment, People, Trade.
  - Lighting modes (Dawn, Noon, Sunset, Night).
  - Procedural canvas synthesis with live telemetry.
- **Historical Archive Tab**:
  - Direct translations of rock edicts, copper plate grants, Greek & Roman travelogues, and Sanskrit/Persian chronicles.

## Launch Instructions

Run:
```bash
start_eventurion.bat
```
Or:
```bash
python -m http.server 8000 --directory eventurion
```
Then open `http://localhost:8000/index.html` in your browser.
